## Editor's notes

**Brand consistency.** The hero shows "Aaavatar" but the product screenshot says "Avatar." Pick one and apply it everywhere — domain, App Store listing, in-app, and footer. "Aaavatar" is more distinctive and ownable; "Avatar" is cleaner but very generic in search.

**Number formatting.** On the English site use `€4.99` (period, not comma). Keep `€4,99` on a future Dutch version.

**Section header was missing.** Added "Three steps to a consistent team." above the feature grid so the section has a clear promise before the cards.

**"Fill up body" → "Fill in missing shoulders".** "Fill up body" reads as broken English; the new line names the actual benefit (cropped LinkedIn headshots often miss shoulders).

**"Easy cost." → "Simple pricing. No surprises."** "Easy cost" is a Dutch-to-English literal translation that doesn't land. The replacement adds reassurance (no hidden fees) which is a known conversion lifter on pricing sections.

**Pricing features.** Your draft used `Feature 1 / 2 / 3` placeholders. The lines above are a reasonable Free vs. Pro split for a tool like this — adjust to match what you actually ship.

**Typo fix.** "colleage's" → "colleague's" (resolved by rewriting the line entirely).
